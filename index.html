What drives the decision

The model separates operating risk from valuation risk.

Returns Below target

Base-case IRR is 18.6% versus a 20% underwriting target.

Debt service Tight

Downside equity At risk

Downside IRR is negative, showing limited protection from the capital structure.

How the model works

Revenue and margin assumptions produce EBITDA and free cash flow. Cash pays required debt amortization and an excess-cash sweep. Exit enterprise value less remaining debt determines sponsor proceeds, MOIC, and IRR.

Files and assumptions

The workbook includes sources and uses, operating cases, debt schedules, returns, and sensitivities. The memo converts the model into an investment recommendation.

Research conclusion

Cash conversion needs context

The screen identifies changes worth investigating, then ties them back to filings. Wesco’s FY2025 growth absorbed cash, MSC’s FY2023 receivables-sale program boosted reported cash conversion, and special charges explain much—but not all—of Grainger’s FY2025 margin decline.

How the platform works
  1. Download public company facts from the SEC.
  2. Normalize annual observations with Python and pandas.
  3. Store values, periods, tags, filing dates, and accessions in SQLite.
  4. Calculate ratios and year-over-year diligence rules.
  5. Render interactive comparisons and filing-based research in Streamlit.
Validation, files, and limitations

A separate validation script confirms all 120 database observations against the cached SEC source, including reporting periods and filing references. Fiscal calendars and business mixes differ, so peer comparisons remain directional.
