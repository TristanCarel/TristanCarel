Behind the model

What the model contains

Historical inputs, operating forecasts, transaction sources and uses, debt schedules, returns, parallel base/downside calculations, exit sensitivity, and an investment dashboard. Source references are retained inside the workbook.

Key transaction assumptions

5.5x entry EV/EBITDA; 4.0x initial leverage; 7.0% blended interest; 100% excess-cash sweep. Base: 5% growth, 52% EBITDA margin, 5.5x exit. Downside: 2% growth, 48% margin, 4.5x exit.

Definitions and important limits

EBITDA is operating income plus depreciation and amortization, rather than company-reported Adjusted EBITDA. Tax, working capital, financing, and cash-sweep assumptions are simplified. Base minimum DSCR is approximately 1.24698x and is below the 1.25x threshold before rounding. This is an educational hypothetical transaction, not a company forecast.

Tools and project process

Excel financial modeling, scenario analysis, debt rollforwards, cash reconciliation, and investment memo writing. AI assistance supported troubleshooting, presentation, and packaging. Interactive results use the packaged model's cached outputs.

Inside the SQL query

A parameterized query joins financial observations to company names and fiscal periods. The preview below follows the company and year selected above and displays saved SQLite results; it does not run a database in your browser.

SELECT c.company_name, f.year, f.metric,
       f.value_usd, f.source_tag, f.filed,
       f.accession, p.period_start, p.period_end
FROM annual_financials AS f
JOIN companies AS c ON f.ticker = c.ticker
JOIN fiscal_periods AS p
  ON f.ticker = p.ticker AND f.year = p.year
WHERE f.ticker = ? AND f.year = ?
ORDER BY f.metric;

Filing dates can be later than the fiscal period because the dataset uses selected annual observations from saved SEC files.

Metric definitions & comparability

Cash conversion = operating cash flow / consolidated net income. Operating margin = operating income / revenue. Free cash flow = operating cash flow − capital expenditures. Free-cash-flow margin = free cash flow / revenue. Trade working capital = receivables + inventory − trade payables. Working-capital intensity = year-end trade working capital / revenue.

MSC's fiscal year ends around August; Grainger and WESCO end in December. MSC FY2022 includes 53 weeks. Business mixes and capex definitions differ. Year-end balances are snapshots, not annual averages, and balance-sheet changes do not directly reconcile operating cash flow.

Data provenance, scope & files

This is a historical screening and research project using public information, not a valuation, prediction, or point-in-time backtesting model. Validation checks completeness and consistency with saved SEC observations, including periods and filing references; it does not establish that every accounting tag is the best analytical choice. Research notes require manual review when data changes.
